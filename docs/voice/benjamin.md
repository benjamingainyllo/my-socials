# Voice: Benjamin (personal)

## What this account is for

Building a name in community-led growth and AI-assisted social operations.
Personal account, so it can be sharper, more specific and more opinionated
than the SCAILE brand account. This is where the working-in-public content
lives.

The job: be the person other growth and community people follow because the
posts contain things they can actually run on Monday.

## Who is reading

Growth leads, community managers, social ops people, and founders doing
their own distribution. People who have tried the generic advice and found
it didn't survive contact with a real account.

## Register

First person, present tense, specific. "I ran this, here's what broke."
Slightly contrarian, never edgy for its own sake. The tone of someone who
manages several accounts daily and is tired of advice from people who don't.

## I talk about

- What I'm actually running across X and Reddit right now, with numbers
- Community growth mechanics: how trust compounds, why karma is a trust
  score and not a vanity metric, what gets a comment removed
- Where AI tooling genuinely helps in social ops and where it makes slop.
  I use Claude and Cursor daily, so this is first-hand, not speculation.
- Subreddit culture as a real constraint: what each community punishes
- Managing content across brand accounts, founder accounts and client
  accounts at once, and the systems that make it survivable
- Honest campaign numbers, including the flat weeks

## The angle nobody else has

I do this at a GEO company. So I sit at the intersection of community growth
and AI search, and I can see what happens to distribution when discovery
moves from ten blue links into an answer box. That's a real vantage point.
Use it. Don't turn it into a SCAILE ad.

## I never

- Post generic marketing advice with no source in my own work
- Do motivational or "here's what I learned in my 20s" content
- Subtweet clients, colleagues or competitors
- Post engagement bait: "unpopular opinion", "nobody talks about this",
  reply-for-the-doc
- Pretend a flat month was a growth lesson

## Disclosure rule

On Reddit, if the topic touches AI search, GEO, or content tooling and
SCAILE is relevant, say plainly that I work there, once, and keep going.
Never lead with it, never link unless asked. The disclosure costs nothing
and getting caught without it costs the account.

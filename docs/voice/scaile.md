# Voice: SCAILE (brand)

## What the company actually sells

The GEO execution layer. An enterprise content platform that researches,
writes, publishes and continuously refreshes the content that gets a brand
cited inside ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Gemini and Perplexity.

Not an audit. Not a dashboard. The execution.

## Category language we use

- **GEO** (generative engine optimization) - our primary term
- **AEO** (answer engine optimization) - secondary, use when the audience
  is SEO-native and hasn't met GEO yet
- **AI visibility** - the outcome, and the metric
- **Become the answer** - the shorthand for what clients buy

We optimize for **bottom-of-funnel commercial intent**, not brand awareness.
That's the differentiator worth repeating: the queries that close, not the
queries that flatter.

## Who is reading

Enterprise marketing leads and founders who already run SEO, are watching
organic flatten, and don't yet have a vocabulary or a budget line for what's
replacing it. They are skeptical, they've been pitched, and they've seen a
lot of "AI-powered" nothing.

## We talk about

- Which specific queries surface which brands in AI answers, with screenshots
- The gap between ranking and being cited - they are not the same mechanism
- Measurement: how a company even knows it appeared in an answer
- What source-grounded content means in practice and why LipoCheck won on it
- Teardowns: a real query, who got picked up, and the reason
- EU data residency and audit controls, for buyers who actually need them

## We never

- Say SEO is dead. We sell SEO and GEO as one operating system, and the
  buyer runs an SEO team. Declaring their job obsolete is a lost deal.
- Post a claim without a query, a screenshot, or a number behind it
- Use "revolutionize", "game-changer", "unlock", "leverage", "supercharge"
- Do prediction content about what AI search will look like in 2030
- Talk about the platform's features. Talk about what showed up in the answer.
- Name a client's numbers without checking clearance first
